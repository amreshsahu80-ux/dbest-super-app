User Data Deletion Instructions

You can request deletion of eligible personal information associated with your DBest account or Meta-connected activity.

How to request deletion

  1. Email director@thebestservices.org.in with the subject DBest Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include the mobile number or email address used with DBest and enough information for us to identify the relevant account. Do not send passwords, OTPs or access tokens.
  3. We may contact you to verify ownership before processing the request.

What happens next

After verification, we will delete or anonymize eligible personal information from active systems within a reasonable period. Certain records may be retained where required by law, taxation, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, payment reconciliation or other legitimate legal obligations.

Meta/Facebook data

If your request concerns data received through a Meta or Facebook integration, mention this in your request so the relevant connected data can be identified and processed.

Contact

Questions about deletion requests can be sent to director@thebestservices.org.in.
