⏱️ Civic Issue Follow-up Schedule

Once an issue is reported, its original reporter may submit one fresh, factual status update when eligible. The schedule starts on the report date, then resets after each accepted update. Severity is assigned by JanSathi rules and may be reclassified only by the verified Project Owner.

🔴 Urgent48 hoursOnly when escalated by verified Owner
🟠 High7 daysRoads, drainage, water and garbage by default
🟡 Moderate30 daysStreetlight issues by default
🟢 Low90 daysOther non-urgent issues by default

Sample cadence examples only; real issue dates and eligibility appear under My Reports after sign-in. Submitting a citizen update does NOT imply government action, resolution, or acknowledgement. No automatic email reminders are enabled yet.

👍 Support counts show how many other registered local accounts endorsed an issue. Support is not an official resolution, verified survey, or national promotion. Sample counts are fictional.

Posts are displayed by selected Village / Town / Block / District / State. National reach is exclusively controlled by the verified Project Owner; there is no publisher-controlled national switch.

What the status labels mean

“DBest received” means the complaint has been saved by JanSathi only. “Submitted to authority” requires actual submission evidence. “Officially acknowledged” requires an official reference number. This pilot does not send complaints to any authority.

📰 Owner · News Editorial Review

New submissions stay private until approval. Publish, reject or withdraw only after independent fact-checking. Every decision requires a written audit reason.

🇮🇳 Project Owner · National Publishing

Only the verified Project Owner can elevate or withdraw a real published item. Every change is audited. Local editors cannot do this.

⏱️ Project Owner · Issue Severity and Follow-up Cycles

Review real civic reports and reclassify severity with a documented reason. This changes the reporter's next eligible update time. All changes are audited.

📍 Project Owner · Public Site Verification and Distance Limits

Only the verified Project Owner can approve a published public issue site or change support distances. Citizen-entered coordinates are NOT verified. Never treat these as a verified home address.
